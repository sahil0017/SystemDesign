# System Design Interview by Alex Xu (Vol 1 & 2)

## Summary

Practical system design interview preparation with real-world case studies. Volume 1 covers fundamentals (load balancers, caches, databases, message queues) and case studies (URL shortener, cat photo sharing, web crawler). Volume 2 covers advanced topics (distributed systems, consistency, scalability) and more case studies (Uber, YouTube, news feed).

## Key Topics

### Volume 1: Fundamentals
- Load balancing algorithms and health checks
- Caching strategies and cache invalidation
- Database scaling: replication, sharding, federation
- Message queues and event-driven architecture
- CDN and edge computing

### Volume 2: Advanced
- Consistency models and trade-offs
- Distributed consensus and coordination
- Stream processing and real-time analytics
- Machine learning system design
- Security and privacy considerations

## Key Takeaways

- **System design is iterative**: Start simple, identify bottlenecks, add complexity incrementally
- **Trade-offs are everywhere**: Every design decision has pros and cons — understand and articulate them
- **Communication matters**: In interviews, explain your thinking clearly and ask clarifying questions
- **Practice with real systems**: Study how companies like Twitter, Uber, and Netflix design their systems

- Best book for system design interview preparation
- Clear diagrams and step-by-step design process
- Covers both fundamentals and advanced topics
- Includes practice problems with solutions

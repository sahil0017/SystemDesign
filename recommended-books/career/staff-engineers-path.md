# The Staff Engineer's Path by Tanya Reilly

## Summary

Guide to transitioning from senior to staff engineer. Covers technical leadership, influence without authority, cross-team projects, mentoring, and measuring impact. Written by a former Google SRE and founder of Level Up Labs.

## Key Chapters

1. **What Staff Engineers Do**: Scope, ambiguity, impact, and time horizon
2. **Technical Strategy**: Defining architecture, evaluating technologies, roadmaps
3. **Influence Without Authority**: Building consensus, navigating politics, communication
4. **Mentoring**: Growing other engineers, creating growth paths
5. **Measuring Impact**: Tracking outcomes, visibility, career progression

## Key Takeaways

- **Staff engineers solve ambiguous problems**: You won't have a clear path — your job is to create one
- **Writing is essential**: Design docs, RFCs, and proposals are your primary tools
- **Influence > Authority**: Persuade through data, relationships, and clear communication
- **Mentoring is force multiplication**: Growing other engineers amplifies your impact
- **Say no to focus**: Protect the team's technical focus by declining low-priority work

## Why Read This Book

- Best book for understanding what staff engineers actually do
- Practical advice from someone who has been through the transition
- Includes exercises and reflection questions for self-assessment

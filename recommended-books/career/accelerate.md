# Accelerate by Nicole Forsgren

## Summary

Research-backed guide to high-performing tech organizations. Based on 5 years of research by DORA (DevOps Research and Assessment), it identifies the practices that separate elite-performing engineering teams from the rest. Covers deployment frequency, lead time, MTTR, and change failure rate.

## Key Chapters

1. **The Four Keys of Performance**: Deployment frequency, lead time for changes, time to restore service, change failure rate
2. **Technical Practices**: CI/CD, trunk-based development, automated testing, infrastructure as code
3. **Organizational Practices**: Psychological safety, team autonomy, data-driven decision making
4. **Leadership Practices**: Creating a learning culture, empowering teams, measuring what matters

## Key Takeaways

- **Elite performers deploy 208x more frequently** than low performers
- **Lead time for changes**: Elite performers deploy in < 1 hour; low performers take 2-6 months
- **Change failure rate**: Elite performers have 0-15% failure rate; low performers have 45-60%
- **Time to restore**: Elite performers restore service in < 1 hour; low performers take > 1 month
- **Culture matters**: Psychological safety and team autonomy are as important as technical practices

## Why Read This Book

- Data-driven approach to engineering excellence — not opinion, but research
- Shows what separates elite performers from average teams with hard data
- Actionable recommendations for both technical and organizational improvements
